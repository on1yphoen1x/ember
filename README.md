# Ember

A Dynamic Island for the MacBook notch. It sits around the camera housing and shows what's playing, with a spinning album art pill and a waveform while music plays. Hover over it to open the full player with a scrubber and playback controls.

On Macs without a notch, the island appears at the top of the screen only while something is playing.

## Install

1. Download `Ember.zip` from the [latest release](https://github.com/on1yphoen1x/ember/releases/latest).
2. Unzip it and drag Ember into Applications.
3. Open it. The first time a song plays, macOS asks to let Ember control Music or Spotify. Click OK.

Ember updates itself from the menu bar icon.

## Supported players

Apple Music and Spotify.
